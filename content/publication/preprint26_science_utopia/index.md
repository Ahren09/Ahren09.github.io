## Overview

SciUtopia is a persistent LLM-agent simulation of academic research ecosystems. Researchers, institutions, collaboration networks, funding, and the literature evolve together across simulated years. The framework connects topic selection, submission, peer review, resubmission, citations, funding, and researcher attrition, supporting controlled studies of how institutional policies and information channels shape scientific activity.
