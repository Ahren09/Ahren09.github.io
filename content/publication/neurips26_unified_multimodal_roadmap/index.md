## Overview

This position paper develops a roadmap for functional unification in unified multimodal models. It organizes the research agenda around five layers: modality-aware input grounding, coordinated representations for understanding and generation, stateful multimodal reasoning, joint optimization, and system-level evaluation. These layers connect architectural choices to the preservation and combination of model capabilities.
