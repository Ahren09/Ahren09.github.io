This study examines how title rewrites affect engagement when YouTube videos are shared on Reddit. A controlled, multi-phase experiment accounts for video popularity, timing, and community norms. The analysis identifies emotional resonance, lexical richness, and community-specific language as characteristics of effective rewrites.
